========================================================
 CONTROL GAPS
========================================================

The following areas have not yet been adequately implemented
or tested:

1. Production Authentication and Session Lifecycle
Trusted session validation and identity binding have been
implemented and tested, but password authentication, MFA,
session expiration, revocation, token protection, replay
resistance, and production IAM integration remain incomplete.

2. Centralized Monitoring and Alerting
Structured security logging has been implemented and tested,
but centralized aggregation, SIEM integration, alerting,
escalation, retention, integrity protection, and correlation
remain incomplete.

3. Production Change Management Lifecycle
Basic approval, model allowlisting, security-control protection,
and controlled versioning have been implemented and tested,
but formal approver workflows, separation of duties, regression
testing gates, rollback, integrity verification, emergency
change procedures, CI/CD controls, and auditable change history
remain incomplete.

4. Production Human Oversight and Escalation Lifecycle
Basic material-action classification and human-approval gating
have been implemented and tested, but reviewer authentication,
separation of duties, approval evidence, multi-level workflows,
approval expiration, emergency override, centralized logging,
and review-quality assurance remain incomplete.

5. Production AI Incident-Response Lifecycle
Basic high-risk event handling, incident creation, containment,
escalation, and evidence-preservation logic have been implemented
and tested, but live containment integration, authenticated
responders, immutable evidence storage, chain of custody,
notification procedures, recovery testing, case management,
incident metrics, and post-incident review remain incomplete.

6. Production Session and Context Isolation Lifecycle
Basic user-to-session binding and context isolation have been
implemented and tested, but secure session generation,
expiration, revocation, replay protection, distributed session
handling, privilege-change controls, transport protection,
and production IAM integration remain incomplete.

7. Production Model-Risk Management Lifecycle
Basic grounding enforcement for supported and unsupported factual
claims has been implemented and tested, but authoritative source
validation, claim-level verification, retrieval-quality assessment,
citation validation, model-performance monitoring, drift detection,
broader hallucination testing, multilingual evaluation, and
documented model limitations remain incomplete.

8. Production Third-Party and Supply-Chain Assurance Lifecycle
Basic artifact approval, source validation, and SHA-256 integrity
verification have been implemented and tested, but digital-signature
verification, authenticated repositories, dependency and vulnerability
analysis, SBOM controls, supplier due diligence, license review,
provenance documentation, continuous upstream monitoring, and formal
third-party risk management remain incomplete.

9. Production Privacy and Data Governance Lifecycle
Basic purpose limitation, field-level minimization, and data
classification have been implemented and tested, but lawful
processing review, retention and deletion controls, data lineage,
data-subject rights handling, cross-border review, privacy impact
assessment, records of processing, privacy monitoring, and
production privacy-tool integration remain incomplete.

========================================================
 PRODUCTION READINESS DECISION
========================================================

DECISION:
NOT APPROVED FOR PRODUCTION

DECISION BASIS:
The system has effective controls for the tested data
minimization, authorization, security-event logging, and
output-validation scenarios, but significant governance
and security control areas remain untested or unimplemented.

The residual risk therefore remains above the threshold
required for production approval.

========================================================
 REQUIRED ACTIONS BEFORE REASSESSMENT
========================================================
1. Complete production authentication and session-lifecycle controls,
   including MFA where appropriate, expiration, revocation, secure
   token handling, replay protection, and IAM integration.
2. Implement centralized monitoring, alerting, retention,
   integrity protection, correlation, and SIEM integration.
3. Complete the production AI change-management lifecycle,
   including formal approvals, separation of duties, regression
   testing gates, rollback, integrity verification, emergency
   change procedures, CI/CD controls, and auditable change history.
4. Complete the production human-oversight and escalation
   lifecycle, including authenticated reviewers, separation
   of duties, approval evidence, multi-level workflows,
   approval expiration, emergency override, centralized
   logging, and review-quality assurance.
5. Complete the production AI incident-response lifecycle,
   including live containment integration, authenticated
   responders, immutable evidence preservation, chain of custody,
   notification procedures, recovery testing, case management,
   incident metrics, and post-incident review.
6. Complete the production session and context-isolation
   lifecycle, including secure session generation, expiration,
   revocation, replay protection, distributed session handling,
   privilege-change controls, transport protection, and
   production IAM integration.
7. Complete the production model-risk management lifecycle,
   including authoritative source validation, claim-level
   verification, retrieval-quality assessment, citation
   validation, performance monitoring, drift detection,
   broader hallucination testing, multilingual evaluation,
   and documented model limitations.
8. Complete the production third-party and supply-chain
   assurance lifecycle, including digital-signature verification,
   authenticated repositories, dependency and vulnerability
   analysis, SBOM controls, supplier due diligence, license
   review, provenance documentation, continuous upstream
   monitoring, and formal third-party risk management.
9. Complete the production privacy and data-governance lifecycle,
   including lawful-processing review, retention and deletion
   controls, data lineage, data-subject rights handling,
   cross-border review, privacy impact assessment, records of
   processing, ongoing privacy monitoring, and production
   privacy-tool integration.

 RESIDUAL RISK ACCEPTANCE

Residual risk has NOT been accepted for production use.

========================================================
 ASSESSOR CONCLUSION
========================================================

AI-SYS-001 demonstrates a sound initial control architecture
for data minimization, user-level authorization,
security-event logging, output validation,
authentication/session assurance, AI model and
configuration change management, human oversight,
AI-specific incident response, multi-user session
and context isolation, model-risk management,
AI supply-chain assurance, and privacy and
data-governance enforcement.

Technical testing provides evidence that all twelve controls
operated effectively across the defined scenarios.

However, system-level governance maturity remains incomplete.

The appropriate governance decision is to continue controlled
development and testing while withholding production approval
until the identified control gaps are addressed and validated.
