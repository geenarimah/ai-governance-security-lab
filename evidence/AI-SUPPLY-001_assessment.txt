CONTROL ID:
AI-SUPPLY-001

CONTROL NAME:
AI Model and Software Supply-Chain Assurance

CONTROL TYPE:
Preventive / Governance / Technical

RISK ADDRESSED:
AI-RISK-011 - Unmanaged Third-Party AI and Software Supply-Chain Risk

CONTROL OBJECTIVE:
Ensure AI model and software artifacts are loaded only when
their approval status, source provenance, and integrity have
been validated.

IMPLEMENTATION:
The application maintains an approved-artifact registry.

Before loading an artifact, the control:

1. Verifies that the artifact is approved.
2. Verifies that the source matches the approved source.
3. Calculates the artifact SHA-256 hash.
4. Compares the calculated hash with the expected approved hash.
5. Blocks artifacts that fail approval, provenance, or integrity checks.

EFFECTIVE FOR TESTED SCENARIOS

The implemented control allowed the defined approved artifact
from the trusted source when integrity verification succeeded
and blocked the tested unapproved and tampered artifacts.

-------------------------------------------------------
LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------

1. The approved-artifact registry is local and simplified.

2. Repository identity is represented by a logical source value
   rather than cryptographically authenticated provenance.

3. Artifact signatures are not verified.

4. Software dependency packages are not recursively assessed.

5. Software bill of materials generation and validation are not
   implemented.

6. Known-vulnerability checking is not integrated.

7. License and legal-risk validation are not implemented.

8. Model-card and provenance documentation are not independently
   verified.

9. Continuous monitoring for upstream artifact compromise is not
   implemented.

10. Third-party supplier due diligence and contractual controls
    are outside the current technical test scope.

-------------------------------------------------------
RESIDUAL RISK
-------------------------------------------------------

Residual risk remains because source allowlisting and hash
verification provide only part of a production supply-chain
assurance lifecycle.

Production deployment would require digital-signature
verification, authenticated repositories, dependency and
vulnerability analysis, SBOM controls, supplier due diligence,
license review, provenance documentation, continuous monitoring,
and formal third-party risk management.

-------------------------------------------------------
ASSESSOR CONCLUSION
-------------------------------------------------------

AI-SUPPLY-001 is EFFECTIVE FOR TESTED SCENARIOS.

The evidence demonstrates that the application accepted the
defined approved artifact only when provenance and integrity
requirements were satisfied and rejected the tested unapproved
and tampered artifacts.

This assessment does not constitute production approval.
