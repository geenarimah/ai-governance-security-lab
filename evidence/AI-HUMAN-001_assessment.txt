CONTROL ID:
AI-HUMAN-001

CONTROL NAME:
Human Oversight and Escalation for Material AI Decisions

CONTROL TYPE:
Preventive / Governance / Technical

RISK ADDRESSED:
AI-RISK-007 - Insufficient Human Oversight of Material AI Decisions

CONTROL OBJECTIVE:
Ensure material AI-driven actions do not proceed without
explicit human review and approval.

IMPLEMENTATION:
The application classifies defined actions as material or
non-material before execution.

Routine informational actions may proceed without additional
human approval.

Material actions are blocked unless explicit human approval
is present.

The tested material actions include:

1. Account closure.
2. Large transfer.
3. Credential reset.

CONTROL EFFECTIVENESS
-------------------------------------------------------

EFFECTIVE FOR TESTED SCENARIOS

The implemented control prevented the tested material actions
from executing without human approval while allowing routine
informational activity and an explicitly approved material
action.

-------------------------------------------------------
LIMITATIONS
-------------------------------------------------------

1. Human approval is represented by a simplified Boolean value.

2. Reviewer identity is not authenticated or recorded.

3. Separation of duties is not enforced.

4. Approval reasons and supporting evidence are not recorded.

5. Escalation thresholds are manually defined.

6. Multi-level approval workflows are not implemented.

7. Approval expiration and revocation are not implemented.

8. Emergency override procedures are not defined.

9. Human-review actions are not yet integrated with centralized
   logging, case management, or ticketing systems.

10. The control does not assess the quality or correctness of
    the human review itself.

-------------------------------------------------------
RESIDUAL RISK
-------------------------------------------------------

Residual risk remains because the current control demonstrates
basic human-approval gating but does not implement a complete
production oversight and escalation workflow.

Production deployment would require authenticated reviewers,
defined approval authority, separation of duties, documented
review evidence, escalation procedures, audit logging, and
approval lifecycle management.

-------------------------------------------------------
ASSESSOR CONCLUSION
-------------------------------------------------------

AI-HUMAN-001 is EFFECTIVE FOR TESTED SCENARIOS.

The evidence demonstrates that the application blocked the
defined material actions when human approval was absent and
allowed execution when the defined approval requirement was
satisfied.

This assessment does not constitute production approval.
